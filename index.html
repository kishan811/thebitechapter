👑 The Food Philosophy

"Cooking should feel like a dance, not a chore."

I started The Bite Chapter as a personal food journal to document recipes that make everyday cooking feel comforting rather than exhausting.

Whether it's our signature smoky charcoal-infused tadka chutney, 10-minute garlic mushroom pasta, or crispy golden cheelas packed with clean protein, my goal is simple: high flavor, minimal dishes, and plating that brings a smile before the very first bite.
